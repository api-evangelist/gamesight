# Gamesight Documentation

> The marketing platform for PC & console games. Gamesight helps game companies build their influencer relationships, understand their marketing, and grow their community.

## Guides

This includes Marketing Attribution for PC and Console games, Cost Aggregation (data-syncing from ad networks into Gamesight reporting), and Incrementality Testing.
